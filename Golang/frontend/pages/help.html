Help and quick start

Everything in this prototype is simulated: no money moves and no real purchase is made. Use the steps below for a quick end-to-end walkthrough.

Start here

  1. Choose an agent, then choose a product. On Shop, choose who is doing the buying at the top of the page, then click any of the twelve product cards. Browsing only prepares the product; press Continue with RAMIFY to deliberately start the purchase-context check. Start with the Autonomous buying agent and Apex Nutrients Magnesium Glycinate. Every check passes, so that agent is allowed to complete the simulated purchase itself.
  2. Read the product result and the agent response. The answer shows the product's own posture first, then what your agent may do with it. When they differ, the reason is written between them.
  3. If a transaction is permitted, use the action shown in the final step. Consumer-style agents can stage a receipt-backed basket line. Procurement-style agents create a separate signed simulated requisition instead. The exact signed receipt controls which path is available.
  4. Now try a case that needs attention. Same agent, but click Greenline Botanicals Ashwagandha — its lab certificate lapsed 90 days before the dataset's snapshot date, and the agent that happily bought the last one stops and asks.
  5. Read the receipt. Under Your receipt you get what a person needs: the product, the reference, when it was sealed, who it was checked for, and the decision in plain English. Press What was found or Technical record if you want the rest.
  6. Answer it. Go to Needs me. Press Why it needs you for the reason in plain English, Compare alternatives if you would rather buy something else, then agree or overrule. Both are recorded.
  7. Verify the recorded history. On Activity, press Verify receipt history.

If you would rather be walked through it, open the interactive tour. It highlights one place at a time and explains why each step exists.

Questions people ask

Am I actually buying anything?

No. Nothing here touches a real shop, a real payment or real stock. The products, brands, sellers, laboratories and prices are invented. It is a working model of how the checking would go, not a shop.

What are the seven checks?

Whether the product could be identified at all; whether there is a recall or advisory against the batch; whether the seller is established for this category; whether the paperwork the category requires is attached; whether that paperwork is still in date; whether the claims made about it come from bodies entitled to make them; and whether any two sources contradict each other.

Each returns pass, review, fail or incomplete. All seven and what each found are shown under What was checked on any result.

Why is "incomplete" not just a fail?

Because missing evidence is not evidence of a problem. If a certificate was never attached, that tells you nothing about the product — it tells you something about the filing. Treating the two the same would either condemn sound products or, worse, teach people to ignore real failures.

Why did my agent stop on something that looks fine?

Because "fine" is two different questions. The product may have satisfied every check and still be something your agent is not allowed to buy — over your spending limit, from a brand you have not approved, from a seller you have no arrangement with.

When that happens the result says so plainly: the objective posture stays where it was, your agent's decision is the stricter one, and the reason between them names your rule rather than the product. On Needs me, Why it needs you spells it out again.

My agent stopped and I still want the thing. What now?

Two options. Compare alternatives looks for something the same agent could actually buy — every candidate is put through the whole assessment for that agent before it is offered, so nothing is suggested that would also be stopped. Or choose Authorise once, which records your name, role, note and one-transaction scope in a separate system-sealed Human Receipt.

Alternatives are never offered against a recall. Routing around a safety finding is not a service.

Can I change what my agent is allowed to do?

Yes. On My agents, press Edit what it may do on any card. You can change its spending ceiling, which brands and sellers it may buy from, whether warnings always come to you, and whether it may complete a purchase without asking.

You can also press New agent to build one from scratch. As you change the settings, the panel at the bottom of the editor shows exactly what that agent would then be able to do.

Could I set up an agent that buys something unsafe?

No, and not because we ask you nicely. There is no setting for it. A recalled product permits one action — stop — for every agent that exists, including any you invent. The editor shows you that: the bottom rows always read cannot buy, whatever you have typed above.

Your settings can only make an outcome stricter. That is enforced in the code, not left to whoever writes the next agent.

What is a receipt for?

Proving, later, that the question was asked and what the answer was. It records what was checked, what was found, what was decided and when, and it is sealed so that any change afterwards shows up.

Press Check this receipt to verify one. Open Technical record and press Test a changed copy to watch a tampered copy get rejected.

What happens if I authorise a stopped item?

A separate Human Receipt records who decided, their role, what RAMIFY found, what the agent did, the decision note, what happens next and the original receipt ID and hash. The original machine receipt is left exactly as it was. The Human Receipt can be read in plain English, verified, downloaded, printed and expanded as full JSON.

A recall is different: it is blocked and never enters this review flow. No agent setting or human-review button can turn a recalled batch into an allowed purchase.

Does the AI decide anything?

No. When the local AI layer is ready, the native Go adapter sends the shopper's words to a Llama 3.1 model running through Ollama on this machine. The model may identify a known catalogue item and later explain an already-sealed receipt. Every trust judgement, policy decision and purchase permission in between comes from deterministic RAMIFY code.

How do I know the local LLM is really running?

Look at the top-right status badge. LOCAL AI · LLAMA3.1 means the live Go + Ollama path is ready. LOCAL · AI FALLBACK means RAMIFY is still working, but request interpretation and receipt explanation are using the safe deterministic fallback. The Technical page shows the same boundary in more detail.

Does this need the internet?

After the RAMIFY Go release is extracted and the optional Llama model has been installed, the demo is designed to operate locally. The RAMIFY server uses loopback, and the optional model connection goes to the local Ollama service on the same machine. Internet access is only needed for first-time Ollama/model downloads, not for the intended presentation flow.

What are the demo cases behind that button?

Seventeen named situations the prototype was built to demonstrate — a recall, advisories, expired and withdrawn certificates, a discontinued product with a named replacement, a seller whose authority is unverified, and a product with nothing on file. Choosing one prepares its product and agent, but RAMIFY still waits for you to deliberately continue.

They are behind a button because a buyer should meet a shop. They are one press away because whoever is presenting should not have to remember which product does what.

Something looks out of date.

Reload the page. The build reference in the footer changes whenever the interface does, so if it does not match what you were told to expect, you are looking at an old copy.

Where things live

Shop
Check a product, read the answer and the receipt, buy it if it passes. The demo cases live behind the button beside the catalogue.
My agents
Who buys for you, one product put in front of all of them at once, and the editor for changing what each may do.
Needs me
Anything an agent stopped on, with the reason in plain English and a way to compare alternatives. The number beside it is how many are waiting.
Basket
Consumer basket lines, signed simulated procurement requisitions and completed simulated orders. Each transaction stays linked to the receipt that authorised it.
Activity
Recent decisions, Action Gate events and signed transaction records in plain English, with independent verification controls for the recorded proof.
