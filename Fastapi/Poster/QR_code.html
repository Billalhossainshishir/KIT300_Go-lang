RAMIFY OS

RAMIFY OS helps a person make a decision step by step. It keeps a clear record of what was chosen, what information was used, and whether the final decision record has been changed or not.

3. Why do we use SHA-256 and Ed25519?

You do not need to understand cryptography to understand the purpose. These tools help RAMIFY check whether a decision record can still be trusted.

Step 1
Make a record

RAMIFY creates a Decision Receipt that stores the important details of the decision.

Step 2
Create a fingerprint

SHA-256 creates a unique digital fingerprint of that receipt.

Step 3
Add a digital signature

Ed25519 signs the record so RAMIFY can later check that it came from the expected source.

Step 4
Check before action

If the fingerprint or signature does not match, the action is stopped.

ReceiptCanonical decision recordStores decision, evidence references and metadata in a stable format.
IntegritySHA-256Creates a digest that changes if the receipt content changes.
AuthenticityEd25519Checks that the receipt was signed by the expected key.
ControlAction GateRequires a valid receipt before the workflow can continue.

Receipt is valid

The digital fingerprint matches and the signature is valid. RAMIFY treats the record as unchanged and allows the workflow to continue.

Receipt was changed

The fingerprint or signature does not match. RAMIFY treats the record as unsafe and blocks the final action or sends it for review.

5. Human review

Sometimes a person may need to review or change a decision. RAMIFY keeps the history instead of hiding the earlier decision.

Original Receipt The first decision stays saved.
→
Human Review A person reviews the case and records the reason.
→
New Receipt A new signed receipt is created and linked to the first one.
This means RAMIFY does not simply overwrite the past. It keeps a clear history of what happened and why.
